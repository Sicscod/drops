# 25 · ✈️ Until I See You

For long distance. A dark night globe made of dotted continents slowly turns, with two glowing points: you, and Delly. A dashed flight path arcs between your cities while a little paper plane flies it, leaving a warm trail. Underneath, a big live countdown (days : hours : min : sec) runs until the day you see each other. Tap the globe to spin it, tap "send a kiss" to send hearts flying along the route to her side. When the countdown hits zero, the two points meet in the middle and open into a heart: "finally." Put your cities and the date in the link and send it to your person. One HTML file, no libraries.

## Change it without coding
```
https://sicscod.github.io/drops/25-until-i-see-you/?name=Delly&from=New%20York&to=Paris&date=2026-12-20T18:00
```
- `name`: your person (default Delly)
- `from` / `to`: your city and hers. About 40 big cities are built in (London, Tokyo, Seoul, Almaty, Sydney...); for any other place write `Town@lat,lon`, e.g. `?to=Bergen@60.4,5.3`
- `date`: the day you meet, e.g. `2026-12-20T18:00`. Without a date (or once it has passed) it plays a short time-lapse demo that ends in the heart
- `hue`: her colour from 0 to 360, e.g. `?hue=200` for blue

## Make it yours
1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) → **Download raw file** (it's the whole project, one file)
2. Open `index.html` and edit the `CONFIG` block: names, cities, date, colour, the lines of text
3. Publish for free: **Settings → Pages → main** (or drop the folder on Vercel)
