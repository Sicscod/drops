# 36 · 🖋️ Ink Drop

A tall glass of water on a soft studio backdrop, shot like a macro photo: refraction edges, a meniscus, slow bubbles and caustic light on the table that takes the colour of the water. Tap and a drop of ink falls in, rings spread on the surface, and it sinks in billowing curls of crimson, ink blue and magenta (a real little fluid simulation). After a few drops the curls drift together and settle into a heart, then "Delly" writes itself out of the swirls, and a line appears under the glass: "you got under my skin". Change the name in the link and send it to your person. One HTML file, no libraries.

## Change it without coding
```
https://sicscod.github.io/drops/36-ink-drop/?name=Delly&msg=you%20got%20under%20my%20skin&hue=348
```
- `name`: what the ink writes
- `msg`: the small line under the glass, e.g. `?msg=always%20you`
- `hue`: main ink colour from 0 to 360, e.g. `?hue=300` for plum, `?hue=215` for blue-black, `?hue=15` for rust
- `auto`: add `&auto` and the ink drops by itself (nice on a screen on a shelf)

## Make it yours
1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) → **Download raw file** (it's the whole project, one file)
2. Open `index.html` and edit the `CONFIG` block: name, message, ink colours, how many drops before it writes, how long it waits, bubbles, sound on/off
3. Publish for free: **Settings → Pages → main** (or drop the folder on Vercel)
