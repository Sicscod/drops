# 32 · 🕯️ Make a Wish

A small chocolate cake in a dark room, close up, with real-looking candles: the flames flicker and sway, light the frosting and the glaze, and bloom softly into the dark. Swipe across a flame (or tap it) and it leans the way you blew, shrinks, lets go of the wick, and a curl of smoke rises while the room gets a little darker. When the last candle goes out, all the smoke drifts together and writes "Delly" in the air, then a small line fades in under it: "my wish is always you". Soft whoosh sounds on every candle, and you can light them again. Change the name, the line and the number of candles in the link and send it to your person. One HTML file, no libraries.

## Change it without coding
```
https://sicscod.github.io/drops/32-make-a-wish/?name=Delly&msg=my%20wish%20is%20always%20you&candles=5
```
- `name`: what the smoke writes
- `msg`: the small line under the name, e.g. `?msg=happy%20birthday,%20love`
- `candles`: how many candles are on the cake, from 1 to 12, e.g. `?candles=7`

## Make it yours
1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) → **Download raw file** (it's the whole project, one file)
2. Open `index.html` and edit the `CONFIG` block: name, line, candles, frosting and glaze colours, candle wax and stripe, flame size, how dark the room gets, sound on/off
3. Publish for free: **Settings → Pages → main** (or drop the folder on Vercel)
