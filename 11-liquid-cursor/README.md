# 11 · 💧 Liquid Cursor

Glowing liquid ink that follows your finger. A chain of metaballs stretches behind every move, throws off droplets when you go fast and swallows them back; tap anywhere and a splash repaints the ink in the next palette (molten, mercury, acid, deep sea, cherry). Leave it alone and it draws figure-eights on its own. One HTML file, no libraries.

**Live demo:** https://sicscod.github.io/drops/11-liquid-cursor/

https://github.com/Sicscod/drops/raw/main/11-liquid-cursor/demo.mp4

## Change it without coding
```
https://sicscod.github.io/drops/11-liquid-cursor/?colors=b026ff,ff5ce1,ffe8ff&sticky=0.12
```
- `colors`: your own palette as three hex colours (deep, mid, highlight), shown first
- `blobs`: how long the tail is (default 16)
- `sticky`: 0.05 runny and wobbly … 0.5 tight
- `hint`: the line at the bottom

## Make it yours
1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) → **Download raw file** (it's the whole project, one file)
2. Open `index.html` and edit the `CONFIG` block: palettes, tail length, blob size, splash, glow
3. Publish for free: **Settings → Pages → main** (or drop the folder on Vercel)
