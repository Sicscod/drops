# 03 · 🐉 Neon Dragon

A glowing Chinese dragon that follows your finger or cursor. One HTML file, no libraries.

**Live demo:** https://sicscod.github.io/drops/03-neon-dragon/

https://github.com/Sicscod/drops/raw/main/03-neon-dragon/demo.mp4

## Use it
1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) → **Download raw file**
2. Open `index.html` in any browser. That's it.
3. On a phone: drag your finger. On a computer: move the mouse. Leave it alone and the dragon flies by itself.

## Customize
At the top of the script:
- `SEGMENTS`: body length
- `HEAD` / `TAIL`: colors (RGB), gold → red by default. Try `[90,200,255]` → `[140,60,255]` for an ice dragon.
