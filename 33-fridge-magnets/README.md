# 33 · 🧲 Fridge Magnets

A close-up of a mint fridge door: brushed enamel with soft window reflections, a chrome handle, a grocery list under a red dome magnet, and chunky glossy plastic alphabet magnets scattered at angles with real shadows. Drag a letter and it lifts off the door (the shadow grows and softens), tilts as you move it, then snaps back with a little wobble and a plastic clack. Drop it near its spot and it clicks into line. After a few letters the rest fly in by themselves, and the door reads "you + me = ♥ Delly", with the heart beating. Change the name and the line in the link and send it to your person. One HTML file, no libraries.

## Change it without coding
```
https://sicscod.github.io/drops/33-fridge-magnets/?name=Delly&line=you%20%2B%20me%20%3D%20%E2%99%A5&hue=158
```
- `name`: the second row of magnets (it also ends up on the grocery list)
- `line`: the first row, e.g. `?line=be%20mine%20%E2%99%A5`; a `♥` becomes a heart magnet
- `hue`: the fridge colour from 0 to 360, e.g. `?hue=200` for sky blue, `?hue=40` for butter, `?hue=350` for rose

## Make it yours
1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) → **Download raw file** (it's the whole project, one file)
2. Open `index.html` and edit the `CONFIG` block: name, line, fridge colour, the grocery list, magnet colours, the spare letters, how many letters before the rest fly in, sound on/off
3. Publish for free: **Settings → Pages → main** (or drop the folder on Vercel)
