# 37 · 🔴 Laser Cat

A chunky hand-drawn cat in a dark living room at 2 am, and your finger is the laser pointer. It tracks the dot with its eyes and head, wiggles its butt before every pounce, skids across the floorboards on fast moves, bonks into the wall with a "!" and a ring of stars, and if you hold the dot still on it, it gives up, sits down and does a slow blink: "...prrr". Every pounce is counted. One HTML file, no libraries, all drawn on a canvas.

**Live demo:** https://sicscod.github.io/drops/37-laser-cat/

https://github.com/Sicscod/drops/raw/main/37-laser-cat/demo.mp4

## Change it without coding
```
https://sicscod.github.io/drops/37-laser-cat/?fur=black&speed=1.3&dot=%2300ff88
```
- `fur`: the cat: `orange`, `black`, `grey` or `white`
- `speed`: how fast it runs, `0.4` (sleepy) to `2.5` (zoomies), default `1`
- `dot`: laser colour as hex, e.g. `%2300ff88` for a green laser

## Make it yours
1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) → **Download raw file** (it's the whole project, one file)
2. Open `index.html` and edit the `CONFIG` block: fur colours, pounce range, how long it wiggles, how hard it has to hit the wall to bonk, the purr text
3. Publish for free: **Settings → Pages → main** (or drop the folder on Vercel)
