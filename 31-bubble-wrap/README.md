# 31 · 🫧 Bubble Wrap

A sheet of bubble wrap with a message printed underneath. Glossy domes catch the light, the plastic has its own wrinkles and sheen, and every bubble you tap or drag across squashes, snaps flat into a crumpled crater and puffs out a little air. Each popped bubble shows the letter printed under it, and after a few sweeps the sheet reads "i'd pop every bubble for you, Delly ♥", then the heart starts beating. There's a pop counter in the corner and tiny pop sounds. Change the name and the message in the link and send it to your person. One HTML file, no libraries.

## Change it without coding
```
https://sicscod.github.io/drops/31-bubble-wrap/?name=Delly&msg=i'd%20pop%20every%20bubble%20for%20you,&hue=355
```
- `name`: the last line, with a small heart after it
- `msg`: the words printed under the bubbles, e.g. `?msg=you%20make%20me%20this%20calm,`
- `hue`: the paper colour from 0 to 360, e.g. `?hue=210` for blue, `?hue=20` for orange

## Make it yours
1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) → **Download raw file** (it's the whole project, one file)
2. Open `index.html` and edit the `CONFIG` block: name, message, paper colour, ink colour, the faint print, bubble size, sound on/off
3. Publish for free: **Settings → Pages → main** (or drop the folder on Vercel)
