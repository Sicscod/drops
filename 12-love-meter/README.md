# 12 · 💗 Love Meter

"How much do you love me, Delly?" She drags the meter up, the heart fills like a glass and beats faster, and it says things on the way ("that's it?", "getting warm"). Let go below 100 and it quietly creeps back up on its own. At 100 the heart pops, hearts explode everywhere and a card lands: "I knew it. 💗 see you tonight?". Change the name and the message in the link and send it to your person. One HTML file, no libraries.

**Live demo:** https://sicscod.github.io/drops/12-love-meter/?name=Delly

https://github.com/Sicscod/drops/raw/main/12-love-meter/demo.mp4

## Change it without coding
```
https://sicscod.github.io/drops/12-love-meter/?name=Delly&msg=dinner%20at%208%3F
```
- `name`: who you're asking
- `msg`: the line on the card at 100%
- `head`: the big line on the card (default "I knew it. 💗")

## Make it yours
1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) → **Download raw file** (it's the whole project, one file)
2. Open `index.html` and edit the `CONFIG` block: name, card text, the comments at each %, how fast it creeps up, colours
3. Publish for free: **Settings → Pages → main** (or drop the folder on Vercel)
