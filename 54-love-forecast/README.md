# 54 · ☀️ Love Forecast

A weather app that only has one variable. Alone, it's −12° and snowing, a grumpy cloud is crying over the city and the forecast says "feels like: lonely · 0% chance of Delly". Swipe the location switch from **alone** to **with Delly** and the weather breaks: the sun pushes the cloud aside, the sky melts from grey to pink and gold, the temperature rolls up to 36.6°, every snowflake turns into a heart, the hourly icons flip to suns and hearts and the chance climbs to "100% chance of Delly". Tap the sun and it giggles. Swipe back and winter returns. One HTML file, no libraries, everything drawn on a canvas (no images).

## Change it without coding
```
https://sicscod.github.io/drops/54-love-forecast/?name=Delly&msg=home&hue=340
```
- `name`: who changes the weather ("with {name}", "100% chance of {name}")
- `msg`: what it feels like when they're here (default "home")
- `hue`: the colour of the warm sky and the hearts, 0–360 (e.g. `?hue=0` red, `?hue=280` purple, `?hue=200` blue)
- `auto=1`: a cursor plays the whole thing by itself (good for screen recordings)

Swipe (or tap) the location switch to change the weather, tap the sun to make it giggle.

## Make it yours
1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) → **Download raw file** (it's the whole project, one file)
2. Open `index.html` and edit the `CONFIG` block: the two temperatures, the labels, the date line, the hourly icons, the colour, sound
3. Publish for free: **Settings → Pages → main** (or drop the folder on Vercel)
