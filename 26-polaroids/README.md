# 26 · 📸 Polaroids

An instant camera on a wooden table at night, under a warm lamp. Tap to print a polaroid: it slides out dark and slowly develops into a little memory, drawn in code (a first sunset, two coffees, city lights at 2am, a moon over the roof), with a handwritten caption. Shake it to develop faster and toss it on the pile. The last one develops into words written on a fogged window: "to many more, Delly ♥". One HTML file, no libraries, no photos.

**Live demo:** https://sicscod.github.io/drops/26-polaroids/?name=Delly

https://github.com/Sicscod/drops/raw/main/26-polaroids/demo.mp4

## Change it without coding
```
https://sicscod.github.io/drops/26-polaroids/?name=Delly&captions=our%20first%20sunset|coffee%20%231|the%20moon%20from%20your%20roof
```
- `name`: your person, used in the last polaroid
- `captions`: one polaroid per caption, separated by `|`. The picture follows the words: sun / sea / beach → sunset, coffee / cafe → two cups, city / night / lights → skyline, moon / roof / stars → moon over a rooftop
- `last`: the words on the last polaroid, e.g. `?last=happy%20anniversary,%20{name}%20♥` (`{name}` is replaced)

## Make it yours
1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) → **Download raw file** (it's the whole project, one file)
2. Open `index.html` and edit the `CONFIG` block: captions, last message, develop time, marker colour
3. Publish for free: **Settings → Pages → main** (or drop the folder on Vercel)
