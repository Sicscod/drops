# 19 · 🎆 Fireworks

A night sky over a sleeping city, and it's yours to light up. Tap anywhere and a rocket climbs from behind the skyline and bursts right where you tapped: peonies, rings, golden willows, crackling glitter, all with real physics (gravity, air drag, trails, flickering embers), additive glow, smoke haze that gets lit by every new burst, and the whole show reflected in the river. Every fourth firework the sparks pull together and write her name in light, hang there, then drift down like real embers. The eighth tap is the grand finale: a full salvo, "Delly" in the sky and one last line, "you light up everything". Change the name in the link and send it to your person. One HTML file, no libraries.

**Live demo:** https://sicscod.github.io/drops/19-fireworks/?name=Delly

https://github.com/Sicscod/drops/raw/main/19-fireworks/demo.mp4

## Change it without coding
```
https://sicscod.github.io/drops/19-fireworks/?name=Delly&msg=happy%20new%20year&hue=200
```
- `name`: the name written in sparks
- `msg`: the last line that appears after the finale
- `hue`: main firework colour from 0 to 360 (e.g. `340` rose, `45` gold, `200` ice blue, `130` green)
- `auto`: add `&auto` and the show plays by itself, no tapping needed

## Make it yours
1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) → **Download raw file** (it's the whole project, one file)
2. Open `index.html` and edit the `CONFIG` block: how often the name appears, which tap is the finale, how long the name hangs, how many sparks, rocket speed, gravity, drag and trail length
3. Publish for free: **Settings → Pages → main** (or drop the folder on Vercel)
