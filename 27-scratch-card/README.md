# 27 · 💌 Scratch Card

A premium lottery-style scratch card, "a little surprise for Delly". Drag your finger across the silver foil and it scratches off for real: ragged coin edges with a lit burr and a shadow, glinting foil dust flying off your finger. Underneath are three matching hearts and the prize: "you won: a date with me. Saturday, 8pm 💌". Once about 60% is scratched, the rest of the foil peels off like a sticker, hearts burst out and a red WINNER stamp lands on the card. Put her name and your prize in the link and send it to your person. One HTML file, no libraries.

## Change it without coding
```
https://sicscod.github.io/drops/27-scratch-card/?name=Delly&prize=a%20date%20with%20me.&when=Saturday,%208pm
```
- `name`: who the card is for
- `prize`: what she wins, e.g. `?prize=breakfast%20in%20bed.`
- `when`: the small line under the prize, e.g. `?when=Sunday,%2010am`

## Make it yours
1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) → **Download raw file** (it's the whole project, one file)
2. Open `index.html` and edit the `CONFIG` block: name, prize, when, how much to scratch before it auto-reveals, coin size, ink and heart colours
3. Publish for free: **Settings → Pages → main** (or drop the folder on Vercel)
