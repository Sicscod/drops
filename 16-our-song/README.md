# 16 · 🎧 Our Song

A tiny music player made for one person: a vinyl spins under a soft glow, the equalizer ring pulses, and love-note lyric lines light up one by one, karaoke style, in time with the progress bar. Tap the heart and it bursts into little hearts. When the song ends: "every song sounds like you". Change the name, the title and the lyrics in the link and send it to your person. One HTML file, no libraries.

**Live demo:** https://sicscod.github.io/drops/16-our-song/?name=Delly

https://github.com/Sicscod/drops/raw/main/16-our-song/demo.mp4

## Change it without coding
```
https://sicscod.github.io/drops/16-our-song/?name=Delly&song=our%20song&lines=you%20walked%20in|and%20the%20room%20got%20quiet|I%20still%20hear%20it&hue=350
```
- `name`: who the song is for
- `song`: the song title (also printed on the record label)
- `lines`: the lyric lines, separated by `|`
- `end`: the line on the last card (default "every song sounds like you")
- `hue`: accent colour, 0–360 (350 rose, 25 amber, 200 blue)

## Make it yours
1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) → **Download raw file** (it's the whole project, one file)
2. Open `index.html` and edit the `CONFIG` block: name, title, lyrics, colour, seconds per line
3. Publish for free: **Settings → Pages → main** (or drop the folder on Vercel)
