# 38 · ✈️ Paper Plane

A note on lined notebook paper, on a dark desk at night: "can't stop thinking about you, Delly", in handwriting. Tap it and the sheet folds itself into a paper plane, crease by crease, with real 3D folds, light and shadows. Flick it and it flies across the room: a loop-de-loop, then a dotted trail that draws a heart, a hammerhead turn at the tip, and a soft landing that spins it back to you. Then it unfolds, the creases stay in the paper, and a new line is waiting: "ok now send this back". Change the words in the link and send it to your person. One HTML file, no libraries.

## Change it without coding
```
https://sicscod.github.io/drops/38-paper-plane/?name=Delly&msg=miss%20you%20already&back=your%20turn%20%E2%9C%88&hue=350
```
- `name`: the name on the note (and in the corner)
- `msg`: the note itself; write `{name}` anywhere to drop the name in
- `back`: the line that appears after it lands (a `✈` at the end is drawn as a little doodle)
- `hue`: the pen colour as a hue (0–360), e.g. `226` blue biro (default), `350` red, `140` green, `280` purple

## Make it yours
1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) → **Download raw file** (it's the whole project, one file)
2. Open `index.html` and edit the `CONFIG` block: the note, the second line, ink colour, the clock in the corner, fold speed, how long the heart takes, how big the plane flies
3. Publish for free: **Settings → Pages → main** (or drop the folder on Vercel)
