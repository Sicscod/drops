# 24 · 🌸 Blossom

A cherry tree grows for her: the branches split and taper out of the ground, thousands of blossoms pop open under a soft dusk moon, and when you drag your finger across the branches the wind blows the petals off. They flutter down, settle on the grass and slowly walk into her name, "Delly", with a little line underneath. Change the name in the link and send it to your person. One HTML file, no libraries.

**Live demo:** https://sicscod.github.io/drops/24-blossom/?name=Delly

https://github.com/Sicscod/drops/raw/main/24-blossom/demo.mp4

## Change it without coding
```
https://sicscod.github.io/drops/24-blossom/?name=Delly&msg=happy%20birthday&hue=345
```
- `name`: the name the petals spell on the ground
- `msg`: the small line under the name
- `hue`: blossom colour, e.g. `345` pink, `0` red-pink, `280` lilac, `40` peach

## Make it yours
1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) → **Download raw file** (it's the whole project, one file)
2. Open `index.html` and edit the `CONFIG` block: name, message, colour, how many blossoms, how fast it grows, `seed` for a different tree
3. Publish for free: **Settings → Pages → main** (or drop the folder on Vercel)
