# 21 · 💫 Two Stars

Two stars, "you" and "Delly", circle each other in deep space, each dragging thousands of glowing dust trails. Drag your finger anywhere to pull them closer: they spin faster, start sharing dust, and when they finally touch there's a soft supernova. The dust settles into a heart-shaped nebula that beats, and "out of all the stars, I found you" fades in underneath. Change the names in the link and send it to your person. One HTML file, no libraries.

**Live demo:** https://sicscod.github.io/drops/21-two-stars/?name=Delly

https://github.com/Sicscod/drops/raw/main/21-two-stars/demo.mp4

## Change it without coding
```
https://sicscod.github.io/drops/21-two-stars/?name=Delly&me=Sam&msg=out%20of%20all%20the%20stars,%20I%20found%20you
```
- `name`: her star (default Delly)
- `me`: your star (default "you")
- `msg`: the line under the heart; the part after the first comma is the big line
- `hue`: her star's colour from 0 to 360, yours follows it, e.g. `?hue=200` for blue and green

## Make it yours
1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) → **Download raw file** (it's the whole project, one file)
2. Open `index.html` and edit the `CONFIG` block: names, message, colour, how many particles, how much dragging it takes
3. Publish for free: **Settings → Pages → main** (or drop the folder on Vercel)
