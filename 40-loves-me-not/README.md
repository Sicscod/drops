# 40 · 🌼 Loves Me Not

A hand-drawn daisy with a little face in a night garden. Tap a petal: it stretches, tears off and flutters down like a real leaf, and the big line flips between "loves me" and "loves me not". The joke: the flower is rigged. When it's losing, it panics ("wait—") and grows a brand-new petal, and the last "not" petal refuses to come off: it stretches like rubber, snaps back ("nope."), and the whole thing ends on "Delly loves me ♥" with a burst of tiny petals and fireflies that gather into a heart. Change the name in the link and send it to your person. One HTML file, no libraries.

## Change it without coding
```
https://sicscod.github.io/drops/40-loves-me-not/?name=Delly&msg=the%20flower%20has%20spoken.%20no%20refunds.&hue=345
```
- `name`: who loves you, in the final line ("Delly loves me ♥")
- `msg`: the small line under the ending
- `hue`: petal tip tint, 0–360 (345 = blush pink, `?hue=200` for blue tips, `?hue=50` for gold)
- `petals`: how many petals it starts with, 3–13 (it still always ends on "loves me")

## Make it yours
1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) → **Download raw file** (it's the whole project, one file)
2. Open `index.html` and edit the `CONFIG` block: name, ending line, petal tint, petal count, what the flower says when it panics and when it refuses, sound on/off
3. Publish for free: **Settings → Pages → main** (or drop the folder on Vercel)
