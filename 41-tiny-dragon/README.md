# 41 · 🐉 Tiny Dragon

A chunky baby dragon that lives on your screen, drawn like a cartoon with wobbly ink lines. It flaps after your finger with a wiggly tail and keeps an eye on the little bug crawling through your code. Tap and it tries to breathe fire... and sneezes a puff of smoke and one sad spark. Tap again: another sneeze. The third tap is the real thing: a huge fire burst that roasts the bug ("fixed ✓"), and then it looks very proud of itself. Hold your finger still and it curls up on it and naps, "z z". Every roasted bug is counted. One HTML file, no libraries, all drawn on a canvas.

## Change it without coding
```
https://sicscod.github.io/drops/41-tiny-dragon/?color=purple
```
- `color`: the dragon: `green` (default), `red`, `purple` or `gold`

## Make it yours
1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) → **Download raw file** (it's the whole project, one file)
2. Open `index.html` and edit the `CONFIG` block: how many sneezes before the real fire, how far the flame reaches, how long you hold still before it naps, how fast the bugs crawl, the sneeze words, the file name at the top
3. Publish for free: **Settings → Pages → main** (or drop the folder on Vercel)
