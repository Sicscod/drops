# 35 · 🔒 Love Lock

A brass love lock on a bridge railing at golden hour, between dozens of weathered locks, with the river and the city lights blurred behind. The lock is engraved "you + Delly" and your date. Drag the three combination dials up or down and they roll with real click detents; set the date and the shackle snaps shut onto the railing with a heavy clunk and a little bounce. Then the key flips through the air into the river, a splash ring spreads, and the line comes in: "locked. no take-backs." Change the name and the date in the link and send it to your person. One HTML file, no libraries.

## Change it without coding
```
https://sicscod.github.io/drops/35-love-lock/?name=Delly&me=you&code=142&msg=for%20as%20long%20as%20this%20bridge%20stands
```
- `name`: the name engraved on the lock
- `me`: the other half of the engraving, e.g. `?me=Sam` for "Sam + Delly"
- `code`: your date as 2 to 5 digits (one dial per digit), e.g. `?code=1402` for 14.02; it's engraved too, so they can find it
- `msg`: the small line under "locked. no take-backs."

## Make it yours
1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) → **Download raw file** (it's the whole project, one file)
2. Open `index.html` and edit the `CONFIG` block: names, the code, the ending line, the corner labels, where the sun sets, the scribbles on the other locks, sound on/off
3. Publish for free: **Settings → Pages → main** (or drop the folder on Vercel)
