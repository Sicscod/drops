# 39 · 🔴 Don't Press

One big glossy red arcade button on a dark panel, with a label that says DON'T PRESS. You know what happens next. Every press, the page gets more desperate: "hey.", then "i said don't.", then the button pops out of its socket and runs away on a coiled cord (catch it), then a little "please 🥺" sign pops out, then the screen glass cracks, then sirens, red strobes and SELF-DESTRUCT IN 3… 2… 1. Then the whole panel falls apart with physics, the label, screws, sign, cracks and the button bounce on the floor, and the page sighs "…fine. you win." with confetti. A smaller button pops back up: "again?". Squash on every press, screen shake, a press counter, little synth sounds. One HTML file, no libraries.

## Make it yours
1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) → **Download raw file** (it's the whole project, one file)
2. Open `index.html` and edit the `CONFIG` block: rewrite the jokes (`lines`, one per press, with how long each stays), the dodge lines and where the button runs to, the sign, the countdown length and speed, the "you win" line, the label tape, sound on/off
3. Publish for free: **Settings → Pages → main** (or drop the folder on Vercel)
