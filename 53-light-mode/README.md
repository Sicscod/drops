# 53 · 🧛 Light Mode

It's 3:00 AM, the editor is dark, and a tiny vampire developer in a cape and a `404 SLEEP` hoodie is happily typing with his DARK ROAST mug next to him. Click the theme toggle and the whole editor flashbangs to light mode: a camera-flash burst, a white-out, and a big LIGHT MODE toast. He screams, hisses, covers his eyes, smokes, sizzles and shrinks into his hoodie while his coffee boils over and the code goes pale. Flip it back and he puffs up to full size, sips his coffee and says "much better." Hover over the toggle one more time and he gives you a look, says "don't.", grabs your cursor in his fist and snaps a padlock on the switch: dark mode. forever. One HTML file, no libraries, all drawn on a canvas.

## Change it without coding
```
https://sicscod.github.io/drops/53-light-mode/?hue=150&time=4:20%20AM
```
- `hue`: hoodie colour, 0–360, e.g. `?hue=150` for green, `?hue=0` for red (default 205, navy)
- `time`: the clock in the corner and in the first code comment, e.g. `?time=5:47%20AM` (default 3:00 AM)
- `auto=1`: a cursor plays the whole thing by itself (good for screen recordings)

Tap the toggle to flashbang him (tap it again to save him). Tap him or his mug to make him sip his coffee. After two flashbangs he locks the toggle; tap the padlock three times and he gives in. On a keyboard: `T` flips the theme, `Space` is a sip.

## Make it yours
1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) → **Download raw file** (it's the whole project, one file)
2. Open `index.html` and edit the `CONFIG` block: the code in the editor, the line he keeps typing, the file name, the clock, the text on the mug and the hoodie, every quip (scream, hiss, sip lines, "don't.", the lock line), the toast, how many flashbangs before he locks it, sound
3. Publish for free: **Settings → Pages → main** (or drop the folder on Vercel)
